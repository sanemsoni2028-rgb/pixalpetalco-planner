Welcome! Let’s get your planner ready.

It takes about 2 minutes. Pick what you need below.

STEP 1

Download your files

  1. On Etsy open You → Purchases, find your order and tap Download files.
  2. Find them in your Files app → Downloads (iPad / iPhone), or your Downloads folder.
  3. Tap Sticker Book.zip once to unzip it. The planner PDFs do not need unzipping.
STEP 2

Choose one planner

Monday start or Sunday start: the pages are identical, only the first day of the week differs. Import the one you prefer and use it all year.

STEP 3

Import into your app

GGoodNotes iPad · iPhone · Mac
  1. Open the Files app and tap the planner PDF.
  2. Tap Share → GoodNotes (or in GoodNotes tap + → Import).
  3. Choose Import as new document, open it and tap HOME.
NNotability iPad · iPhone · Mac
  1. Open Notability and tap the + button.
  2. Tap Import and choose the planner PDF from Files.
  3. Open the new note and tap HOME.
NNoteshelf iPad · Android tablets
  1. Open Noteshelf and tap + (or Import).
  2. Choose the planner PDF from your Files / Downloads.
  3. Open it and tap HOME.
XXodo Android · iPad · Windows
  1. Open Xodo and tap the planner PDF in Files.
  2. Turn on reading / link mode if a tap draws a mark.
  3. Tap HOME and enjoy your planner.

App menus change a little between versions. If a button has a different name, look for Import or Open PDF. Any PDF app that supports hyperlinks works. You need a note-taking / PDF-annotation app to write on the pages.

IMPORTANT

Links not opening?

Quick test: tap HOME → tap MAR at the top → tap a date → tap HOME again.

If a tap draws a dot instead of jumping

Your pen is catching the tap. Switch to Reading (read-only) mode (in GoodNotes: the pen icon with a line through it), or tap links with your finger and set Apple Pencil to draw only.

Still nothing?

Press and hold the link. If you see Open link, tap it. Then update your app to the newest version.

Re-import cleanly

Delete the planner from the app, save the PDF to Files (not a cloud preview) and import it again.

Keep the page order

Links jump to page positions, so please don’t re-order pages. If you delete pages you don’t need, links to those pages will stop working.

STICKER BOOK

Add stickers & widgets

  1. Unzip Sticker Book.zip. Stickers are sorted into folders (medals, washi tape, sticky notes, labels, icons, flowers, widgets…).
  2. In your app tap + → Photos / Image (or drag from Files) and choose a PNG. The backgrounds are transparent.
  3. Pinch to resize, drag to move, copy & paste to reuse. Drop widgets into the dashed INSERT ZONES on week pages.
TASKS & CHALLENGES

Make it stick

The 3-minute task routine

  1. Brain dump into the dotted box on the Task Hub.
  2. Sort into Quick wins, Today’s focus and Big projects.
  3. Pick your top 3 on today’s Day page.
  4. Tick & reward: tick tasks, stick a DONE sticker, enjoy your reward.

Challenges

Open the Challenge Hub, pick one of 12 trackers (7, 14, 21 or 30 days), colour one circle a day, and stick a medal at each milestone. Never miss twice: if you skip a day, just colour the next one.

FAQ

Good to know

Can I print it?

It is made for digital use on a tablet. You can print pages for yourself, but it is not set up as a printable planner.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, to view and tap around. Writing is easiest on a tablet with a stylus.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes, on all of your own devices. It is for personal use only. Please don’t share, resell or upload the files.
